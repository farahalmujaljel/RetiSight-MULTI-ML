# Synthetic Model Card

## Name

MULTI-DEMO-01 synthetic logistic-regression response demonstrator.

## Status

`SYNTHETIC EXPERIMENT - NOT CLINICAL VALIDATION`

## Intended Use

Engineering demonstration of the multimodal pipeline, grouped validation, model comparison, reporting, and inference contract.

## Not Intended For

Clinical decisions, treatment recommendations, clinical deployment, or claims about anti-VEGF performance.

## Inputs

Clinical-only uses baseline systemic and eye measurements. Clinical + OCT adds baseline OCT biomarker flags. Follow-up measurements, endpoint timing, treatment totals, and outcome-derived fields are excluded.
