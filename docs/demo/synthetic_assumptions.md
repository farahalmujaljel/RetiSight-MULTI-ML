# Synthetic Assumptions

The generator uses broad demonstration ranges for DME-relevant age, diabetes duration, HbA1c, renal function, ETDRS visual acuity, CST, OCT biomarkers, injection frequency, loss to follow-up, and six-month response.

These assumptions are illustrative and literature-informed at the level of plausible ranges, not estimated from source-owner verified outcome data. They must not be cited as clinical associations or treatment-effect estimates.

No real OLIVES identifiers, OCT-DME visit timing, treatment histories, or longitudinal trajectories are copied into the synthetic cohort.
