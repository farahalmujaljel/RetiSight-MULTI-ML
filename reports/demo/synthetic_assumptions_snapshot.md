# Synthetic Assumptions

Title: SYNTHETIC EXPERIMENT - NOT CLINICAL VALIDATION

The generator uses broad, literature-informed DME demonstration ranges for age, ETDRS visual acuity, central subfield thickness, diabetes duration, HbA1c, renal function, OCT fluid and structural biomarkers, anti-VEGF injection schedules, and six-month visual response.

These are illustrative engineering assumptions, not estimated clinical parameters. Stochastic noise and missingness are intentionally included so labels are not perfectly separable.

Endpoint window: 150-210 days; target day: 180.
Eligible modeling eyes: 1274.
